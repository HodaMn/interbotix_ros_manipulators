For fixing the serial number of the ports, so that the robots don’t get mixed up! 
Connect one by one, then check if connected generally:
ls /dev | grep ttyDXL
ls -l /dev/ttyUSB*

retrieve the serial number by: 
udevadm info -a -n /dev/ttyUSB0 | grep serial   (for next ones would be USB1…)

since we have two separate PCs (for master and slave), we will use usb 0 and 1 of both PCs
open the file below:
sudo nano /etc/udev/rules.d/99-interbotix-udev.rules

write the leader commands in the master pc and the follower commands in the slave pc:

# Leader U2D2
SUBSYSTEM=="tty", ATTRS{serial}=="FTA7NOU8", ATTR{device/latency_timer}="1", SYMLINK+="ttyDXL_leader"
SUBSYSTEM=="tty", ATTRS{serial}=="FTA7NNBN", ATTR{device/latency_timer}="1", SYMLINK+="ttyDXL_leader_2"

# Follower U2D2
SUBSYSTEM=="tty", ATTRS{serial}=="FT89FLCF", ATTR{device/latency_timer}="1", SYMLINK+="ttyDXL_follower"
SUBSYSTEM=="tty", ATTRS{serial}=="FT88YSCI", ATTR{device/latency_timer}="1", SYMLINK+="ttyDXL_follower_2"


After checking the ports and USBs, use these launch files in their corresponding PCs: 
Leader PC: 
    ros2 launch interbotix_xsarm_puppet leader_pc.launch.py
    
Follower PC: 
    ros2 launch interbotix_xsarm_puppet follower_pc.launch.py
